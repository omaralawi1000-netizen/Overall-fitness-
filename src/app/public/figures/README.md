# Exercise figures

Rendered clay figures with the working muscle lit, one clip per lift (`<lift>.mp4`) and a still of its first frame (`<lift>.webp`).

- `pushdown`: Triceps pushdown, from old Aven's clip 12 ("figures-pushdown-clip12-body", v2 with the fleck fix), cropped to the figure (512 × 640) and re-encoded as H.264.
- Missing, still to render: bench press, incline dumbbell press, overhead press, lateral raise. Until a lift has a clip here, its explainer shows the equipment picture from `../hero/`.

The body is derived from Z-Anatomy (https://www.z-anatomy.com), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). These clips are adaptations of it and are shared under the same licence. The credit is shown in Settings.
