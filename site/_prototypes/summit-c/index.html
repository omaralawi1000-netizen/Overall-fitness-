Summit C: the whole app, organised

One mountain panorama is the wallpaper for every tab. Each tab has the same three places: a big number on the picture, one line from the Coach, and one frosted tray of widgets.

Try:

  • Change tabs: the mountain slides with you.
  • Tap the water drops, the food chips, the sleep and weight tiles.
  • Start Push and log a set: watch for the record. Then rest, minimise, come back.
  • Tap the Coach button (bottom right), then the mic.
