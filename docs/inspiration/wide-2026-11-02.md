# Wide inspiration search (2 Nov 2026, no prototypes)

Omar paused building and asked for a search all over the internet. Stills are in the shared folder `aven2/inspiration-wide-2026-11-02/` (copyright: never in git, links only). Phone board with ♥ / ✕ buttons: https://claude.ai/artifact/Mafnuqn84i2C5ohymPGkdG (votes saved in db collection `wide`, doc id = item id below). Only his ♥ go into `keepers.md`.

## What was searched, and what worked
- Fitness apps on the App Store (about 70 apps, 5 screenshots each) and Dribbble fitness searches (about 800 shots): nearly all the same lime-on-black or purple-gradient look he already rejected. Almost nothing was taken from there, except lifting-specific ideas.
- Beyond fitness: Behance (car HMI, wearables, concepts), Teenage Engineering, Nothing, Poolsuite, Porsche/Polestar, game UI, Strava, Suunto.
- Did not work: Bing image search (keyword-fuzzy, returned film posters), YouTube thumbnails (clickbait, no in-video frames), Pinterest (login wall), Awwwards (502), gameuidatabase (403). So game UI is thin: Persona, Forza, Destiny and Gran Turismo screens were looked for but not usable as stills.

## Ideas the set points at (to test, not decided)
- Numbers as physical readouts: LED, LCD, dot-matrix, analogue needle (PONY, Baroness, Porsche, Nothing X).
- Controls that are objects: dial, keys, pills, plates (Lintu, PunchKit, glass pills, Stacked).
- Richness from material, not photo or gradient: chrome, glass, tactile grey.
- A record or finish as something you hold: card, results board.

## Stills

### Hardware and instruments
- `punchkit-grid` [PunchKit](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/punchkit/id6785249847) (App Store): A sampler built like a Teenage Engineering box: grey keys you press, an orange state, no gloss. A set pad that feels like a key, not a card.
- `punchkit-edit` [PunchKit](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/punchkit/id6785249847) (App Store): Same app, the edit view: a waveform and sliders in plain hardware grey. Shows richness without gradients or glass.
- `poolsuite-player` [Poolsuite FM](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/poolsuite-fm/id1514817810) (App Store): A whole app made as a small retro machine with a screen, buttons and a vinyl. One object, one personality, nothing like a Today screen.
- `nothingx-ring` [Nothing X](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nothing-x/id1568033706) (App Store): Dot-matrix type and a red capsule ring on pure black. A rest timer or set ring that looks like a device readout.
- `nothingx-dial` [Nothing X](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nothing-x/id1568033706) (App Store): A dial with a dot you drag, a red pill for the chosen mode. Colour sits in the control, which matches his rule 4.
- `lintu-dial` [Landing UI/UX Concept: Neumorphism & Glassmorphism](https://www.behance.net/gallery/256344657/Landing-UIUX-Concept-Neumorphism-Glassmorphism) (Behance): A soft-moulded circular dial with small buttons around it. A weight wheel you turn with your thumb, built as a physical object.
- `suunto-month` [Suunto](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/suunto/id1230327951) (App Store): The month as dots, sized by effort and coloured by sport. A history that reads at a glance and is not a bar chart.

### Cars and cockpits
- `pony-led` [PONY HMI GUI Design](https://www.behance.net/gallery/119691861/PONY-HMI-GUI-Design) (Behance): A segmented red-orange LED speed readout, huge. Weight or reps as a lit display with weight, not a thin number.
- `baroness-moto` [Baroness Motorcycle Dash](https://www.behance.net/gallery/200506247/Baroness-Motorcycle-Dash) (Behance): A motorcycle dash with a red LED gear and big numerals on black. Racing-instrument contrast, one hot colour.
- `porsche-dial` [Porsche HMI — Instrument Cluster Concept](https://www.behance.net/gallery/194363481/Porsche-HMI-Instrument-Cluster-Concept) (Behance): A single analogue needle dial with 91 in the middle, almost no chrome. Calm, premium, one number.
- `ev-ring-cluster` [Modern Automotive EV - HMI Design by AmazingUI](https://www.behance.net/gallery/237697177/Modern-Automotive-EV-HMI-Design-by-AmazingUI) (Behance): A blue ring cluster with the speed inside and soft glass around it. Shows a ring that holds one big number without being a fitness ring.
- `next-gen-cluster` [Next-Gen Automotive HMI & Instrument Cluster Design](https://www.behance.net/gallery/220737373/Next-Gen-Automotive-HMI-Instrument-Cluster-Design) (Behance): An instrument cluster with a warm orange glow behind the numbers. The glow comes from the data, not from the background.
- `polestar-charge` [Polestar](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/polestar/id1451196635) (App Store): A charge screen with a giant orange 50% and 250 km that fills the panel, cut off at the edge. A rest or progress number allowed to be too big.
- `dribbble-cluster` [Car UI Dashboard - Dark Mode ⚡️ 3d automobile car car panel ](https://dribbble.com/shots/21022910-Car-UI-Dashboard-Dark-Mode) (Dribbble): A car cluster in dark mode, speed 147 in a thin face with a car outline. Number first, everything else small.
- `dribbble-lcd` [Gait Analysis Mobile iOS App Design android app app design d](https://dribbble.com/shots/27769274-Gait-Analysis-Mobile-iOS-App-Design) (Dribbble): A giant LCD-style 308 on teal with a pressure strip under it. Big seven-segment digits as the whole screen.

- `glass-pills` [Fitness/Health Tracking App UI - iPhone Duo mobile app](https://www.behance.net/gallery/255810703/FitnessHealth-Tracking-App-UI-iPhone-Duo-mobile-app) (Behance): Chunky glass pills in blue, orange and red, each holding a number. A tappable set or stat that looks like a candy lens.
- `future-ageing` [UI/UX for Wellness Aging Care Mood & Meds Tracking app](https://www.behance.net/gallery/242023175/UIUX-for-Wellness-Aging-Care-Mood-Meds-Tracking-app) (Behance): A blue-violet app with raised glass capsule shapes. Shows depth that comes from shapes, not from a photo.
- `liquid-number` [RunAway — Running Tracker Mobile App UI ai b2b crm dashboard](https://dribbble.com/shots/27496213-RunAway-Running-Tracker-Mobile-App-UI) (Dribbble): A number in a hand-held phone with a liquid yellow gradient filling behind it. The colour follows the value, close to his rule 4.
- `ring-hub` [AI Wellness Dashboard UI — Smart Ring Health Tracking Web Ap](https://dribbble.com/shots/27445363-AI-Wellness-Dashboard-UI-Smart-Ring-Health-Tracking-Web-App) (Dribbble): A smart ring hero on a warm dark landscape, with data widgets around it. A rich dashboard that is built around one object.

### Scores, cards and results
- `football-cards` [Sports Posters Design // Season 24 Vol.#2](https://www.behance.net/gallery/214179725/Sports-Posters-Design-Season-24-Vol2) (Behance): Collectible athlete cards and poster graphics with colour fields and big type. A personal record could be a card you hold.
- `race-winner` [Car Racing Game || UI Design car race design figma game game](https://dribbble.com/shots/24905847-Car-Racing-Game-UI-Design) (Dribbble): A race results board in yellow with winner stars and a car cut-out. A finish screen with a real ceremony, not a share card.
- `strava-bodymap` [Strava: Run, Bike, Walk](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/strava-run-bike-walk/id426826309) (App Store): An orange body map on black with a weekly volume total. Muscles that light up as you train.

### Lifting-specific
- `stacked-plates` [Stacked - Workout Tracker for](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stacked-workout-tracker-for/id1116989938) (App Store): A barbell drawn with real plates, 225 and a reps stepper under it. The weight is a thing you see, not a number you read.
- `gravl-sets` [Gravl: AI Personal Trainer](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gravl-ai-personal-trainer/id6450921637) (App Store): Set rows with a sliding bar for each weight, a green tick, an effective-set count. Shows progress inside the set row itself.
- `rep-pulse-map` [Muscle Map — Android app concept for gym beginners](https://www.behance.net/gallery/256910519/Muscle-Map-Android-app-concept-for-gym-beginners) (Behance): A body map to pick muscles, a clean list below. Cream and black, warm and calm, nothing neon.
- `lumen-anatomy` [Lumen: Health Monitoring Dashboard with 3D Anatomy UI](https://www.behance.net/gallery/257927825/Lumen-Health-Monitoring-Dashboard-with-3D-Anatomy-UI) (Behance): A real-looking anatomy figure beside sleep and heart-rate cards. The body as the hero image, not a stock model photo.
- `gym-orange` [Gym Workout App](https://www.behance.net/gallery/248537291/Gym-Workout-App) (Behance): A black and orange gym routine card with a barcode strip, built like a gym pass. A workout card that feels like a ticket.

### HUD and light
- `kinetiq` [Kinetiq: AI-Powered Dance & Movement Analysis App 3d ai app ](https://dribbble.com/shots/27802647-Kinetiq-AI-Powered-Dance-Movement-Analysis-App) (Dribbble): A dancer drawn as glowing particles on black, with a ring around the number. Light that traces the body.
- `eclipse-hud` [Modern HUD Scenes & Elements design elements hud hud element](https://dribbble.com/shots/24357982-Modern-HUD-Scenes-Elements) (Dribbble): An orange eclipse ring on a dark console with fine tick marks around it. A rest ring that feels like an instrument.
- `moto-hud` [Moto HMI](https://www.behance.net/gallery/232270557/Moto-HMI) (Behance): A neon green motorcycle HUD seen from above, with a small number grid. Dense and alive, shows how much detail a small screen can carry.
- `hud-fx` [3D Motion Reel](https://www.behance.net/gallery/210481813/3D-Motion-Reel) (Behance): A burst of pixels and colour used as an opening. A new-record moment that is a surprise rather than a medal.
