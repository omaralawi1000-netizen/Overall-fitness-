Every test, prototype and rejection, newest first: what Omar picked, what he rejected, and why, in his words (Omar, 2 Nov 2026).

- **How it differs from `keepers.md`:** keepers lists only the ideas he approved, to build from. This log is the whole history, with the noes and the reasons, so each round can learn from the last.
- **Before every new round:** read this log, then write two or three lines under "What it suggests now" and tell Omar.
- **After every test, prototype or rejection:** add a dated entry. His words go in quotes. Our reading of them is marked "Reading".
- **Rounds alternate:** a wide search (Sonnet), then one small polished slice (Opus).

## RETIRED (9 Nov 2026, 21:58): the "old Aven ingredients" look
Asked whether design threads should keep using it, Omar said: "No, delete that." The look (near-black room, one soft glow, clear glass, italic serif names, thin numbers, one orange thing to tap, the orb) is **not his taste anymore and not a rule**. Never feed it to a design thread, a judge or a prototype brief. Everything below that praises it is history only. Italic serif titles, which he picked in the taste test, are not part of this retirement; they stand or fall on his latest words.

## What it suggests now (2 Nov 2026, before the bar-picture workout screen)
- **One seamless picture beats panels.** His Today notes ("seamless", "a blurred gradient … like Oura", "make it make sense") and now the picture that blurs at the bottom point the same way: one continuous image is the identity, and boxes on top of it break it.
- **The picture has to mean something.** "Make it make sense": a pretty image that says nothing is decoration. So the picture is the equipment for this set, and it changes with what he does.
- **He cleared the slate for this one.** "Ignore everything I said was good" means Heat, the keepers and the clips are not the brief this time. The constants still hold: minimal but impressive, the depth one tap away, never empty.

## What it suggested before that (2 Nov 2026, before the Heat workout screen)
- **Build on what he already approved, not new concepts.** Six fresh frost ideas got "not really my style". His hearted clips and Heat's light are the fundamentals; every moment on this screen comes from one named clip.
- **Calm on top, depth one tap away.** "Empty" and "minimal but impressive" together mean that the main view stays quiet and the information lives behind small details that open in place.
- **Copy nothing from the old app's structure.** He showed it for the depth it holds, then said we can do it "in a better way".

## What it suggested before that (2 Nov 2026, before the frost round)
- **Plain is the sin, and Heat is the only workout look that escaped it.** Every flat or tidy round died ("plain", "ordinary", "like those"). Heat's one hot light from below survived. So this round keeps Heat's light and type and changes the material and the idea, not the palette.
- **He wants frost he can see.** Frost only shows when something bright and shaped sits behind it. Smooth gradients behind glass read as a tinted card. Every screen this round puts something bold behind the glass (a giant number, a sun, molten blobs, stacked panes).
- **Colour is a theme, not the identity.** He wants themes ("it's going to be another color if I want anyways"), so the idea has to hold in every colour. Each screen ships with six themes and a switch.
- **Motion stays small and honest.** Springs, rolling digits and one signature per screen. No imitations of his clips.

## What it suggested before that (2 Nov 2026, before the wide workout round)
- **He judges looks fast and moments slowly.** Whole looks get a quick no; single moments get hearts. So this round shows four whole workout screens, each different in layout, and asks per screen what he likes.
- **Light beat dark, but "plain" is the real enemy.** Daylight beat Volt, yet both were "plain". Richness (light, depth, material) matters more than light vs dark. This round has two light and two dark, so he can say.
- **A workout screen has to look like a workout.** The premium still read as a Today screen. Each version now answers "what is the weight, how many reps, where am I, what's my record" with gym things (drums, plates, set rows, a keypad), not a hero gauge.
- **Motion is a keeper, but ours is far from his clips.** Keep it light and only where it can match the clip. Look and layout lead.

## What it suggested before that (2 Nov 2026, after he named his motion language)
- **He has a language now, in his own words: physical and continuous.** Numbers with weight, things that transform, big decisions made with the hand, colour inside controls, a quiet Coach, calm screens with one big number. Build from that, not from the older map.
- **Atmosphere is out as a signature.** Skies and room-filling light were "maybe" twice and then dropped. The glow stays as background, not as the event.
- **Show him one finished moment, not more options.** Next: one small polished slice (the workout) in this language, then one change per round.

## What it suggested before (2 Nov 2026, after the taste test)
- **Pictures can't decide most of it.** 8 of 11 pairs got "neither". Several of his notes say "it depends" more than "no": themes, a day that "can change dynamically based on me", rest "could be good, but also, vice versa". Settle these by feeling them on his phone, inside a real moment, not with another A/B of stills.
- **Firm so far:** italic serif names, and the Coach showing up as light that fills the screen (he'd still explore). Data you can feel (water that tilts) is a yes for later, for hydration.
- **Treat as open, not as noes:** rest, tick, finish, the big number, logging. The rejections there are of those two versions. Several look like execution (Heat's flat colour field, a reading app's bloom out of context), not the idea, because he hearted similar ideas in the motion library.
- **Tension to watch:** themes vs "fewer knobs, done perfectly" (his brief). Old Aven grew 8 palettes. One theme done perfectly first, more later.
- He rejects whole looks quickly but loves single moments. Show him moments.
- **After the Pick list (2 Nov):** he picks mechanics he can picture using (trays, rolling digits, card to page, records as objects) and leaves atmosphere (skies, light floods) at "maybe". Prove atmosphere inside a real moment before building on it.
