# VISION: Aven 2

## In one line
A coach in your pocket that knows you, notices what matters, and does the work with you. Logging, plans, charts and
the watch are how it sees you and how you act together.

## What makes it unlike any fitness app
Fitness apps are loggers with a chat box bolted on. Coaching apps (Bevel, Whoop, Gentler Streak) attach a line of text to
a score. Aven 2 turns it round: **the Coach is the app**.

- **It knows you, from you.** A new person talks to it for two minutes, in Danish, English or a mix, by voice or text:
  their days, their sport, their goal, what's hard. It keeps that as memory they can read and edit, and keeps learning
  from what they log, change and undo. Nothing about any one person is built in. Omar is the first test case, not the
  default.
- **It speaks first, only when it matters.** Most days it says one thing in the morning, or nothing. It chooses its
  tone the way a real coach does: warm for a win, casual for a normal day, hard when progress stalls or the same miss
  repeats.
- **It acts.** "Move pull to Thursday", "200 g skyr and two eggs", "I need 2 kg for wrestling by December": it does
  the thing, shows exactly what changed, and gives you Undo. It never gives advice text when it could do the thing.
- **It tells the truth.** Every number it says comes from your data, worked out by the app, not guessed by the AI: real
  wins, plain misses, stalled lifts, whether you're on pace. When the data can't show something, it says so.
- **It looks and moves like nothing else.** Frosted glass with real depth, calm springs you can catch mid-motion, and
  one orb that listens, breathes and answers. Premium and quiet, with the boldness spent on a few moments that matter.

## Who it's for
Anyone a friend hands it to (Omar, 27 Oct): gym regulars, people in other sports, and total beginners, on Android or
iPhone, with their own AI key or none.

- **With a key** (Gemini free tier, or OpenAI): the full Coach.
- **With no key:** logging by tap, voice-to-text, barcode, all tracking and charts, and the plain-numbers Coach notes
  the app can compute itself. The app says honestly what a key would add.
- **Android with the Aven 2 app wrapper:** the watch (Health Connect), the rest timer on the lock screen and watch, and
  a Coach that can speak first while the app is closed.
- **iPhone, and Android without the wrapper:** everything else. A web app can't wake itself up, so there the Coach
  speaks first when you open it, never in the background. It says so plainly, with no pretending.

## A day with it
Omar's real week is used as the example. For a friend, every line would come from their own onboarding and data.

**Morning, 06:05.** He opens the app before the bus. The orb wakes, and one line arrives under the date: *"Push today
after work. Bench 82.5 × 8 is the target, 2.5 kg up on last week. Down 0.4 kg this week, right on pace."* Nothing else
asks for attention. If there's nothing worth saying, there's no line.

**A friend's first open.** No forms. The Coach says hi and asks one thing at a time: what you do, how often, what
you're after. Each answer becomes a memory card you can see. Two minutes later it shows a first week of training and
food targets as a preview: *Apply*, or say what to change. With no key, the same questions come as big tap answers.

**Mid-workout, 15:40.** Sweaty hands, phone on the bench. The set he's on is outlined, with last time's numbers in it.
He taps the big check: it answers on the very next frame, the row lights, the rest timer starts in the floating frost
bar, and the next set moves into view. Beat last time and a small gold "+2.5 kg" rises and fades. He can also say
"8 at 80" to the orb. It logs the set and says it back.

**The rest timer.** The bar fills calmly. In the last three seconds each second swells and the phone buzzes, then it
turns "go" and names the next set. On Android with the wrapper it lives on the lock screen and the watch, with
Done ✓.

**After training.** Finish. The summary counts up the time, volume and sets, names what moved, and sets next time's
targets. If a lift hit a new best, that gets its own moment: the one place in the app that's allowed to be loud.

**Logging food.** He says *"200 gram skyr og to æg"* or types it, snaps the plate, or scans the barcode, all from one
place. The Coach fills in the items as an estimate where it has to, and the totals land in the day ring with Undo.
Protein behind at 19:00? *"130 g of 220. Skyr and a chicken dinner closes it."*

**A bad night.** The watch says 5 h 10. The morning line changes: *"Short night. Train anyway, you always do. Get
protein in early, it's the day you snack most."* It never says "train lighter" to someone who told it not to.

**A stalled lift.** *"Rows stuck at 70 × 8 for four sessions."* It decides, case by case, whether to suggest a change
(a reset, a swap, more food), shows the change as a preview, and it's one tap to apply or ignore.

**A goal reached.** *"94.4 kg. You said mid-December and it's the 9th."* It shows the real numbers, marks the goal done in
memory, and asks one question: hold here, or what's next?

**The same miss, again.** Third evening over this week: *"That's three this week. It's the 20:00 snacking, not dinner.
Want me to move 300 kcal to after 20:00 so the plan matches how you actually eat?"* Plain and harder, never shaming.

**Sunday.** One short review: what happened, against what you said you wanted, and one focus for next week, with Apply.

## The principles every decision follows
1. **Coach first.** If the Coach can do it from one sentence, that beats a button. Buttons stay for what's faster by
   hand, like ticking a set.
2. **True numbers only.** The app computes, the AI words it. No claim without data behind it, and no invented facts
   about the person.
3. **It acts, you stay in control.** Every change is visible and has Undo. Big changes are a preview first. Memory is
   always readable and editable.
4. **Quiet until it matters.** Few screens, few knobs, few messages. Boldness is spent in one place per screen and on the
   signature moments, and everything around it stays calm.
5. **For anyone, on their phone.** Nothing is hardcoded about one person. It works with no key, on iPhone and Android, in
   Danish and English, and at 120 fps on a sweaty hand at the gym.

## The one fixed rule
It never helps with starving, skipping meals, fasting to make weight, water cuts or purging, whatever anyone says or
whatever memory holds. It says no once, briefly, and offers the safe way.
