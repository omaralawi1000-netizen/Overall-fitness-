# MatrAIx check for the Coach exam (12 Nov 2026)

Question: does MatrAIx (github.com/MatrAIx-ai/MatrAIx-Persona-8B, arXiv 2608.04205) help the Coach exam? Research only,
nothing installed, $0. Read: the repo README, docs (persona, application, validation), the Hugging Face dataset card,
the arXiv abstract. Not read: the full paper, the code, any run.

## What it is
A toolkit that turns "persona" records into LLM agents that play a user against a survey, a chatbot, a website or an app,
then scores the run. A persona = 1,290 multiple-choice traits. Public set: about 1M personas (60 % from real-world
text such as Wikipedia, Stack Overflow, Amazon reviews, GSS; 40 % synthetic). Runs on Python, uv and Docker.

## Useful (small)
- **Positive/negative probe pairs** (`persona/validation`): same task, two users who differ in one trait; a check that
  the behaviour differs. For us: same question to two fixture people that differ in one thing (age 16 vs 34, no watch
  vs watch, Danish vs English) and a code check that the Coach's answer changes the right way. No judge needed, so no
  judge bias. Worth adding to `exam/v2`.
- **Simulated user that talks back** for multi-turn cases. We have 10 hand-scripted ones; an LLM user could vary them.
  Costs model calls, so only after the cheap-model plan's Gate A.

## Hype or not for us
- "8.3 billion personas" is sampled records, not people. The paper's own claim: not a replacement for real people.
- The 1M set is almost all Stack Overflow, Wikipedia, Amazon, survey data. No fitness, food or training traits.
  656 of 1,290 traits filled on average, many rows far fewer. Nothing there to pick a lifter from.
- **Licence: research, non-commercial only.** Using it, even a subset, in a commercial product or paid service is not
  allowed. The app is meant to be released one day, so keep it out of the repo.
- It does not fix judge bias. Personas are LLMs, scoring is LLM-judged too. Their own check: persona behaviour shown
  in 91.5 % of 400 trials, judged by LLMs.
- Heavy setup (Docker, WSL on Windows) for what a small TypeScript script already does in `exam/v2/people.ts`
  (14 people, fixed seed).

## Score and call
4/10 for Aven. Fine research, wrong domain. Do not install, do not import the dataset.
Keep: the probe-pair idea. Our fixture people already cover the spread (teen, 58-year-old, nurse on shifts, runner,
beginner). Diversity gap that matters is real humans: 3-5 testers, still open (see `docs/COACH.md`).
