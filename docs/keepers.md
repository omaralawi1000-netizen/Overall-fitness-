Ideas that proved themselves in a prototype **and** that Omar said he liked. Nothing goes on this list by default:
not from earlier looks, not from research, not from a judge. Only Omar's explicit yes puts an idea here (Omar, 1 Nov 2026).

This list does not choose a direction. After Omar picks a spine, use only the keepers that fit it.

After each prototype round, ask Omar what he liked and what he didn't, and update this list from his answers.

Each entry: **what it is** · **where it came from** (round, direction, screen) · **why it worked** (in his words where possible) · date of his yes.
