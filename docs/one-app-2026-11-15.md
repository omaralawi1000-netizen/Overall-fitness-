# One app from the best of 15 Nov (Aven One)

Asked (Omar, 15 Nov 2026, 21:08): "Between all of the things we created, all of the artifacts, take every good thing out of everything and put it into one amazing one." Then (21:09): "you can just plan it and see what's good in each."

Prototype: `site/_prototypes/one/`. This doc is the plan: what each design gave, what went in, what was left out and why.

## The rule that decides everything: one spine, two jobs
A mashup of 20 looks would be worse than any one of them. So there is one spine and every graft has to fit it.
- **Today is the sky.** Summit A (PR #120): one real photograph that follows the real Aarhus sky (sun height, Open-Meteo weather, season), calm light frost on top, one bold number. It is his taste family (Oura, 12 Nov) and the only Today a fresh judge scored 8.
- **The workout is its own world.** Omar, 15 Nov 05:53: "Do not replicate the today screen to the workout screen." It starts from Ridge (PR #123, my pick of the four workout options): your sets build a mountain range over last week's dotted one. Drawn, dusk-coloured, no photo, almost no glass. The photo comes back only for a record and at the finish, so the sky means "you earned this".
- **One app is held together** by the same type (Bricolage Grotesque numbers, Hanken Grotesk text), the same white buttons, the same Coach voice and pop-ups, and the same springs.

## What the finished prototype does
- **Today:** the live sky, "85 kg" hero, Start Push, a sleep / protein / weight sheet whose tiles grow into cards, a weather-aware Coach pop-up with Start now / Later, the sky sheet. Food and Health in the dock open as cards grown out of the tab. A Coach button opens a sheet with real actions and Undo.
- **Workout:** race against Tuesday, giant kg × reps with plus and minus, the bar with the plates to load, the ridge, one Log button and a mic. Logging: the numbers fly into a new peak that rises; a chip says how it compares with Tuesday.
- **Rest:** the Log button becomes the rest bar, the weight becomes the countdown in the same place, mist fills the valley and drains as time runs out, and breathes. The next set's plates slide on while you rest. "Set 3. Go." grows out of the clock.
- **New best:** the dusk drops away to the summit in full sun, a medal flips in, chips pop, and "Keep going" plants a flag on that peak, which stays.
- **Alive everywhere (from Paper):** drag anywhere on the sky or the ridge and the layers move by depth, then spring back; the ridge drifts slowly by depth while you train; tap one of your peaks and it lifts and tells you that set against Tuesday's; plates tilt on and off the bar; rolling digits fade at the edges instead of spilling.
- **Finish:** hold to finish. The sky comes back with today's ridge laid over it and the totals rolling in; Today then shows "Push, done".

## Left out on purpose, and why
Left out above, per row. The general reason: each of them is a second identity (paper, ceramic, plants, stars, dials, Swiss grid) and the brief is one app, not a gallery.
